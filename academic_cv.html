Research Interests

My doctoral research interests converge at the intersection of Real-Time Interactive AI Systems, Virtual Worlds & Serious Games, and Voice-Enabled Multimodal Agent Architectures. Grounded in rigorous graduate foundations of Signal Processing and Machine Learning, my work focuses on:
Real-Time Interactive AI & Serious Games: Building intelligent non-player characters (NPCs) and dynamic behavioral scaffolding inside interactive virtual environments (Unity 3D) via event-driven WebSockets, replacing static scripted game trees with adaptive agency.
Voice-Enabled Multimodal Agent Architectures: Sub-second streaming conversational loops (orchestrating speech recognition, multi-agent LLM reasoning, and low-latency voice synthesis), natural turn-taking dynamics, and dynamic persona modulation for clinical assistive therapy (Autism Spectrum Disorder / ASD).
Signal Processing & Applied Machine Learning Foundations: Mathematical grounding in stochastic processes, statistical signal estimation, temporal state sequences, edge computer vision, and systematic evaluation of multimodal foundation models (Vision-Language Models).
