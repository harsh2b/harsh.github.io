Grounded in my mathematical foundation in Signal Processing and Machine Learning, I design low-latency, event-driven agent architectures that perceive streaming inputs, navigate conversational turn-taking, and adapt behavior in clinical and educational virtual environments.

Peer-Reviewed Publications

IEEE ICSCCC 2026 · Conference Paper

LLM-Powered Real-Time Voice Agent for Task-Guided Practice in Serious Games for ASD

Harsh Kumar Bhojak, Arun Khosla, Neha Sharma
Proceedings of the IEEE 5th International Conference on Secure Cyber Computing and Communications (ICSCCC), 2026
Abstract: Serious games provide structured behavioral interventions for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), yet static instructional engines frequently lead to task abandonment. We present an event-driven speech-to-speech conversational architecture integrating AutoGen, Gemini, and Unity via low-latency WebSockets. The system dynamically adapts its instructional scaffolding to the child's real-time behavioral state, demonstrating a 30% improvement in task completion rate without modifying foundational game rules.
