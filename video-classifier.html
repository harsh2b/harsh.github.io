Sub-Millisecond Multimodal Video Router for Content-Adaptive Chunking

Heavy foundation models (VideoMAE, 7B VLMs) are too slow and cost-prohibitive for high-throughput video indexing gateways ($>2.4$s per stream). VisionChunk deploys Kinetic Gated Temporal Attention MIL over sparse keyframe embeddings ($576$-d) and kinetic motion deltas ($8$-d) to dynamically dispatch incoming video streams to optimal chunking policies in under 1 millisecond on CPU (0.806 Macro-F1, 73.91% Top-1 Accuracy).

PIPELINE STAGE 1
Sparse Multimodal Sampling
Instead of decoding 30 FPS (18,000 frames for 10 minutes), uniformly samples 8 sparse keyframes and computes inter-frame pixel differences ($|I_t - I_{t-1}|$) and Sobel spatial edge gradients in <1.5 ms without GPU optical flow.
PIPELINE STAGE 2
Gated Attention MIL Pooling
Two-branch attention mechanism ($a_t = \tanh(Vh_t) \odot \sigma(Uh_t)$) learns temporal saliency across the 8 timestamps, suppressing static background frames and focusing on high-information event bursts.
PIPELINE STAGE 3
Confidence-Gated Escalation
Implements speculative routing: clear predictions ($P \ge 0.55$) instantly authorize adaptive chunking policies on CPU, cutting cloud ingestion costs by ~70% while reserving heavy VLMs only for rare ambiguous edge cases.
